Attached are the timing curves I pulled from luna-sysmgr's card view. Throw-to-close feels right at 280 ms with the original ease-out, but a card landing in a stack wants a little more overshoot.

Three things I'd like to keep from the original:

  • Cards keep their rounded corners while they scale, even mid-gesture.
  • A flick past half the screen height always closes, whatever the speed.
  • Stacks fan out by 24 px, not a percentage, so phones and tablets match.
