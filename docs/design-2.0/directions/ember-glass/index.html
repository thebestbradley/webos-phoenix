Ember Glass

Ember's layout, type and ember accent, made of glass: every card, pane, bar and sheet is frosted glass over the phoenix wallpaper, as in Aurora Glass. Each column of a tablet screen ends in a toolbar with Enyo 2's slider at its left, and the gesture light is Onyx Reborn's longer bar with fading ends, lit gold to ember.
