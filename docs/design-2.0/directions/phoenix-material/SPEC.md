# Phoenix Material

**The idea.** Material 3's tonal system made webOS: every colour in the UI is generated from the
wallpaper (Material Color Utilities), on opaque tonal surfaces with expressive shapes and bold type,
so each phone looks like its owner's. webOS stays the frame: cards are the unit of everything, the
app menu tab hangs from the status bar, Synergy shows one person across many accounts, and glass
appears only where webOS had it (status bar, app menu, notifications).

**From Onyx:** the segmented RadioGroup (now an M3 connected button group), the dark toolbar's
icon buttons, affirmative/negative button colours (now `primary` / `error`), groupbox headers (now
coloured group labels over segmented list groups). **From webOS:** rounded cards with visible corners,
the app menu tab (top left, rounded lower corner), the dark curved-glass status bar, the gesture
area with its light, notifications at the bottom (banner, then the dashboard strip), Just Type at the
top of the card view, card stacks, the quick launch dock, Synergy's merged contacts and transport picker.

## Tokens
**Colour (dynamic).** `SchemeTonalSpot`, contrast 0, seeded from the wallpaper: `QuantizerCelebi` +
`Score` offer four seeds (shown as swatches in Settings); the user's pick seeds the scheme
(`assets/palettes.css`, generated by `tools/gen_palette.mjs`). Account and calendar colours are fixed
hues **harmonised** to the seed (`customColor`, blend on). Now playing re-themes from the cover.

**Type:** Figtree (SIL OFL 1.1), variable 300-900, Open Sans fallback. Display 45/52 w500; headline
L 32/40 w700, M 28/36 w700, S 24/32 w650; title L 22/28, M 16/24, S 14/20 (w650); body L 16/24,
M 14/20, S 12/16; label L 14/20 w600, M 12/16, S 11/16. **Shape:** 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 28 (cards,
dialogs, panes), 32, 48, full (buttons, search, chips' pills). **Spacing:** 4 pt grid; 16 phone / 24
tablet gutters; list rows 64-76. **Elevation:** tonal steps (surface → container-lowest…highest) plus
shadows e1-e5 (`0 1 2 / 0 1 3 1` … `0 12 28 6`). **Glass:** frame `rgb(4 8 10 / .38)` + `blur(28px)
saturate(1.7)`; menu/notification `container-high 74-80%` + same blur. **Motion:** M3 Expressive
springs: spatial default (damping 0.9, stiffness 700) for card throw and panes, fast (0.9, 1400) for
buttons and shape morph (pressed buttons go squarer), effects (damping 1) for colour and fades.

## Components
Pill buttons (filled, tonal, outlined, text, error); 56 px FAB and extended FAB; M3 switch with the
check in the thumb; 18 px checkbox; slider with a 16 px track, bar handle and stop dot; wavy progress
for playback; filled text field; 56 px search pill; connected button group; tabs with a 3 px
indicator; segmented list groups (outer 20, inner 4, 2 px gaps); dialog (28 radius); glass app menu
(square corner under the tab); glass banner with actions; avatars with **account badges** (one disc
per linked account) and presence; transport chip in the compose bar; draggable pane handle (Enyo's grabber).

## Building it
- **Enact:** dynamic colour with `@material/material-color-utilities` (Apache-2.0) at wallpaper change
  in the shell, published as `--md-*` custom properties through the existing `PhoenixDecorator`/theme
  variables (the luna `wallpaper` setting already knows the file). Components as Enact/Sandstone-free
  React components on `ui/` primitives; springs via CSS `linear()` easings generated from the spring.
- **Ionic:** map `--md-*` onto `--ion-color-*`, `--ion-background-color`, `--ion-item-background`;
  shapes via `--border-radius`; the glass bits are plain `backdrop-filter`.
- **Flutter:** `ColorScheme.fromImageProvider` (or `material_color_utilities`, Dart) → `ThemeData(useMaterial3: true)`;
  `CustomColor` harmonisation for accounts; `BackdropFilter(ImageFilter.blur(28, 28))` for the frame;
  wavy progress with a `CustomPainter`, the connected button group as a `Row` of buttons with per-position shapes.

**GPU cost and fallback.** Live blur is limited to the status bar, gesture area, app menu and
notifications (three small, mostly static layers): cheap on mid-range GPUs. On a Raspberry Pi (no
live blur): a pre-blurred copy of the wallpaper made when it is set, sampled under the glass
(`background-attachment`-style offset), or a flat `surface-container` at 92%. Scheme generation runs
once per wallpaper change, off the UI thread (45-140 ms in Node on a 128 px thumbnail here).

## Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)
Generated schemes keep M3's tone pairs: across both wallpapers and themes the worst text pairs are
primary on surface-container 5.5:1, on-primary on primary 6.5:1, on-surface-variant on
surface-container-highest 7.2:1, the tonal containers 7.2:1 or more, account colours on surface 6.2:1.
Text on glass, measured on the blurred wallpaper's brightest point under each bar: status bar 10.0:1
(tablet, Lily Pads) to 20:1; app menu tab 5.8:1 (light) and 7.9:1 (dark); card view Just Type 4.8:1.
Touch targets 48 px; state is never colour alone (checks in switches and segments, labels on
transports). Reduce motion swaps springs for 150 ms fades.
