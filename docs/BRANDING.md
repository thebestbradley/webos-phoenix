# Branding ideas

Ideation, not decisions. Written 29 September 2026 from the owner's
first ideas and the discussion that followed. Nothing here is chosen, and no
name is used publicly until it has been searched (see [Before choosing](#before-choosing)).

## The idea: an OS name and a UI name

Palm split the two: **webOS** was the operating system, **Luna** and the
card view its interface. The owner's proposal keeps that split, so one OS can
carry the classic and the modern interface, and a smaller device can carry a
lighter one.

| Product line | OS name (idea) | UI name (idea) | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Phones and tablets, classic | **PreOS** | **Phoenix UI 1.x** | The 1.x line: the original webOS look and feel ([ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md#two-lines-1x-and-20)) |
| Phones and tablets, modern | **PreOS** | **Phoenix UI 2.0** | The 2.0 revamp, with docking to monitors and TVs ([CONVERGENCE.md](CONVERGENCE.md)) |
| Watch or pendant (small screen, lighter and faster) | **PixiOS** | **Bennu UI 1.0** | A new, small-screen variant |
| VR / XR | *open* (the owner's placeholder was "webXROS") | *a mythical bird tied to many dimensions* | A later, immersive variant |

**Why these names.** Pre and Pixi were Palm's phones (the Pre, 2009, and the
smaller Pixi, 2009), so fans recognise them at once, and the Pixi was
Palm's small, light device, as the watch would be. Phoenix is the project's
name: webOS rising again. **Bennu** is the Egyptian heron-like bird that
inspired the phoenix myth: smaller, earlier, the same lineage, a good name
for the small device.

## Birds for the XR variant

| Bird | Story | Why it fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Simurgh** (the favourite so far) | Persian. In Attar's *The Conference of the Birds*, thirty birds (*si murgh*) search for the Simurgh and find that together they are it | Many in one: a world made of many views, which is what an immersive interface is |
| **Ziz** | Jewish legend: a bird so large its feet stand on the earth while its head reaches the sky | Spanning realms; short and memorable |
| **Fenghuang** | The Chinese phoenix, a sign of harmony between heaven and earth | A sibling to Phoenix |
| **Huma** | Persian: a bird that never lands and lives its whole life in the air | A floating interface |

## Risks found so far

1. **Pre and Pixi are Palm product names.** HP sold the Palm trademarks in
   2014 to a company tied to TCL
   ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palm,_Inc.),
   [PhoneArena](https://www.phonearena.com/news/Palm-name-returning-to-smartphones-Alcatel-OneTouch-rumored-to-have-purchased-brand-from-HP_id64225)).
   Whether the "Pre" and "Pixi" registrations are still live was not
   confirmed. Even lapsed marks can draw a complaint when the new use leans
   on the old product's reputation, as these names would on purpose.
2. **"Pre-OS" is a technical term** for the pre-boot firmware environment
   (UEFI and similar), which makes "PreOS" hard to find in searches.
3. **"webXROS" is close to "webOS"**, LG's trademark
   ([LEGAL.md](LEGAL.md#name-and-trademarks)), and "WebXR" is the name of a
   W3C web standard. A name of our own is safer, for example the bird alone.

## Fallbacks that keep the theme

If the Palm names are not usable, name the OS after the fire or the nest
and keep the birds for the interfaces. Placeholders only, not searched:
**Ember OS** running Phoenix UI, **Spark** running Bennu UI.

## Before choosing

- Search the USPTO, EUIPO and WIPO registers for every candidate (OS names,
  UI names, the XR bird), in the software and device classes.
- Check domain names and the main app stores and social handles.
- Ask a trademark lawyer for a quick opinion on Pre and Pixi before any
  public use.
- Record the decision in [LEGAL.md](LEGAL.md#name-and-trademarks) and here.

Until then the project stays **webOS Phoenix** in the repository, and
"webOS" is used only to describe history and compatibility.
