**Synergy** brings your accounts together: contacts from every account
appear as one list, linked into one person each, and all your calendars
show in Calendar.

Add an account in the **Accounts** app (or from an app's own
preferences). Today Phoenix syncs contacts and calendars with **CardDAV
& CalDAV** servers: iCloud, Fastmail, Nextcloud and others. Use an app
password where your provider offers one. Google, Microsoft and chat
networks are planned.
