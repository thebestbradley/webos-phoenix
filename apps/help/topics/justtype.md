In card view or the launcher, just **start typing** (or tap the search
pill at the top). Just Type looks everywhere at once:

- **Launch:** apps whose names start with what you typed.
- **Contacts:** people, to call, text or email.
- **Content:** your tasks, voice memos, calendar events, emails and these
  help topics.
- **Quick actions:** start something with the text you typed: *New
  Memo*, *New Task*, *New Voice Memo*, *New Event*, *New Email*.
- **Search the web** with Google, Wikipedia and others.

Tap the Just Type preferences (at the end of the results) to choose what
it searches.
