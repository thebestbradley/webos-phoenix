- **The clock** and the **alarms** are the two buttons at the bottom.
- **Set an alarm:** on the alarms page tap **New Alarm**, then choose the
  time, its name, how often it occurs (*Once*, *Daily*, *Weekdays* or
  *Weekends*) and its sound, and tap **Done**.
- **Turn an alarm on or off** with its switch; tap it to change it, or
  swipe it aside to delete it.
- **When an alarm rings**, tap **Snooze** to hear it again later, or
  **Dismiss**. It rings even when Clock is closed.

*Preferences* in the app menu sets the clock face (*Analog 1*, *Analog 2*,
*Digital*), how long a snooze lasts (5 to 20 minutes), whether alarms
ring when sounds are muted, and a volume that rises as an alarm rings.
