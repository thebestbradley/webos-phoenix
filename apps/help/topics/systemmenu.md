Tap the **top right of the status bar** (the battery and signal icons)
to open the system menu:

- **Battery** level and the date.
- **Brightness** slider.
- **Wi-Fi**, **VPN** and **Bluetooth**: tap to see networks and devices,
  or to turn them on and off.
- **Airplane Mode**, **Rotation Lock** and **Mute Sound**.

Tap anywhere outside the menu to close it. The full settings for each
are in the launcher's Settings tab.
