Each setting has its own icon in the launcher's **Settings** tab, as on
webOS: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Airplane Mode, Screen & Lock, Sounds &
Ringtones, Date & Time, Language & Region, Accessibility, Location
Services, Emergency Info, Device Info and Updates.

The quickest switches are in [the system menu](topic:systemmenu).
