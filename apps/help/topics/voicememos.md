- Tap the **red button** to record. Pause and resume as you like; **Stop**
  saves the memo.
- Tap a memo to play it. **Transcribe** turns it into text, on the device;
  tap a sentence of the transcript to play from there.
- **Search** finds memos by title and by what was said.
- Share a memo by email or text, rename it, or delete it.
