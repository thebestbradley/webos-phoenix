- Tap a folder to open it; tap a part of the path at the top, or swipe
  back, to go up.
- **Select** files by holding one; then copy, cut, delete, rename or
  "Open with". **Paste** appears at the bottom.
- **Open** a file: pictures in the viewer, text in the editor, `.ipk`
  packages in the installer, everything else with the app that knows it.
- Favourite folders are on the star at the bottom.
