- **Dial:** type the number on the dial pad and tap the green dial
  button. As you type, the matching contact appears above the number.
  Tap the dial button with no number to bring back the last one you
  called.
- **Hold 0** for `+`, **hold 1** for voicemail.
- **Call Log** and **Favorites** are at the bottom. Tap an entry to call
  back.
- **During a call:** mute, speaker, keypad (for menus that ask you to
  press a number) and hold. The red button ends the call.
- A second call can be answered while the first waits on hold; tap the
  bar at the top to swap.

Calls from the lock screen are limited to emergency numbers; see
[The lock screen](topic:lockscreen).
