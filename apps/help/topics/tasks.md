- **Today**, **Upcoming** and **Overdue** gather tasks from all your lists.
- **Add a task** at the top of any list; tap a task to edit its notes, due
  date, priority and reminder.
- **Tick** a task to complete it. The check button hides completed tasks.
- A **reminder** comes as a notification; open it to snooze or finish.

From Just Type, *New Task* starts a task with what you typed.
