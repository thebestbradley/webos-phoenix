In **Settings > Accessibility**:

- **High contrast** makes text darker and outlines stronger in the apps.
- **Reduce motion** makes cards, the launcher and the lock screen appear
  and go without animating.
- **Mono audio** and **Captions** are saved for the audio service and the
  video player.
