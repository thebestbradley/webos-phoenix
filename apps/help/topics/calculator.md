- **Calculate** with the digits and **+**, **–**, **×**, **÷** and **=**.
- **%** after **+** or **–** takes that percentage of the first number
  (200 + 10 % adds 20); otherwise it divides by 100.
- **AC** clears everything and starts again.
- **The memory:** **M+** adds the number shown to the memory and **M–**
  subtracts it, **MR** brings the memory back, **MC** clears it. While
  something is in it, the display shows *M:* and its value.
- **Copy** the result, or **Paste** a number, from the app menu.
