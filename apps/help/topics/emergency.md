In **Settings > Emergency Info** you can write down what a medic should
know: your name, date of birth, blood type, conditions, allergies and
medications, whether you are an organ donor, and the people to call.
Pick those from your contacts.

**On the lock screen,** anyone can tap **Emergency Call** on the PIN pad:

1. The emergency dial pad calls emergency numbers only (`911`, `112` and
   the ones for your region). Tap the dial button with nothing typed to
   fill in the main one.
2. **Medical ID** shows what you wrote, and calls your emergency contacts.
3. **Cancel** goes back to the PIN pad.

Turn off **Show when locked** to keep it private.
