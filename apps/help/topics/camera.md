- Tap the **shutter** to take a picture. The switch next to it changes to
  **video**; tap the shutter again to stop recording.
- **Flash:** tap the flash icon at the top left for auto, on or off.
- **Your last shot** is at the bottom left. Tap it to open it in Photos.

Pictures and videos are saved in `/media/internal/DCIM`.
