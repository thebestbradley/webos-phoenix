To unlock, **drag the padlock up** into the ring. If you set a PIN or a
password (Settings > Screen & Lock), the PIN pad asks for it next.

- **Incoming call:** the padlock becomes a phone. Drag it up to answer.
- **Notifications** show on the lock screen if you allow it.
- **Emergency calls:** on the PIN pad, tap **Emergency Call**. You can
  call an emergency number, and see the owner's **Medical ID** and call
  their emergency contacts, without unlocking. See
  [Emergency Info](topic:emergency).
