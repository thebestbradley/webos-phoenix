When something happens (a text, a reminder, a missed call) a **banner**
slides across the bottom of the screen. It then waits as a small icon in
the **notification area**.

- **Open the dashboard:** tap the notification area. Each notification
  has its own row.
- **Act on one:** tap its row to open the app at that message or task.
- **Dismiss one:** swipe it sideways off the dashboard.

Urgent things, like an incoming call or an alarm, come as a **popup
alert** with buttons to answer, snooze or dismiss.

With **Show when locked** on (Settings > Screen & Lock), notifications
also show on the lock screen. See [The lock screen](topic:lockscreen).
