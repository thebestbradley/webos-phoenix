- **Your mail:** *All Inboxes* and *All Flagged* gather every account's
  mail; under them are your favorite folders, then each account's folders.
  Tap a folder to list its messages, and a message to read it.
- **Check for new mail** with the refresh button below the list. While an
  account updates, the list says so.
- **Write a message** with the new message button. Add people in *To*,
  *CC* and *BCC*, attach files with the paper clip, and tap **Send**.
  *Save As Draft* in the app menu keeps it in Drafts; *Discard* drops it.
- **Reply, reply all or forward** from the buttons below a message.
- **File a message** with *Move to Folder*; **delete** it with the trash
  button. Deleted mail goes to the account's **Trash**; *Empty Trash* in
  the app menu empties it.
- **In a message,** the flag button flags it and *Mark As Unread* marks it
  unread again. *Select Items...* acts on several messages at once.
- **Search** with the field above the list.
- **Open Email in New Card** (app menu, while a message shows), or hold a
  message in the list, to read it in a card of its own.

Accounts, signatures and the new mail sound are in *Preferences &
Accounts* in the app menu. To add an account, see [Accounts](topic:accounts).
