summary: Lock the phone with a PIN or a password
keywords: security, passcode, pin, password, lock, secure unlock

---
In **Settings > Screen & Lock**, under **Secure unlock**, choose
**Simple PIN** (at least 4 digits) or **Password** (at least 4
characters). You type it twice.

To change it, or turn it off, you need the current one. The lock screen
asks for it after you drag the padlock up.
