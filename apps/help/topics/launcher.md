The **launcher** holds every app. Swipe up twice in the gesture area (or
tap the launcher button at the right of the dock) to open it, and swipe
up again to close it.

- **Tabs:** Apps, Downloads and Settings. Swipe sideways or tap a tab.
- **The dock** (quick launch) at the bottom keeps four apps within reach
  in card view.
- **Rearrange:** hold an icon until it lifts, then drag it. Drop it on
  another tab to move it there, or on the dock to put it in the dock.
- **Delete** an app you installed: hold its icon and tap the delete badge.
  Built-in apps cannot be deleted.

Tap **Done** when you have finished rearranging.
