Every app you open is a **card**. Swipe up in the gesture area to see
them side by side: that is **card view**.

- **Switch apps:** in card view, slide the cards left or right and tap
  the one you want.
- **Close an app:** flick its card up and off the top of the screen.
- **Stacks:** cards that belong together, like an email you are writing
  and your inbox, stack up. Slide sideways to go through a stack.
- **Rearrange:** hold a card until it lifts, then drag it along its stack.
  Drag it to the edge of the screen to move it out of its stack and into
  the next one.
- **Open the card in the middle:** swipe down in the gesture area, or tap
  it.

Nothing is lost when you switch: an app keeps its place until you close
its card.
