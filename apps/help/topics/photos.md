- **Albums** are the folders with pictures: Camera Roll, Downloads and so on.
- **Viewer:** tap a picture to see it full screen. Swipe sideways for the
  next one; tap once to show the title and the menu.
- **Share** a picture by email or text, **set it as the wallpaper**, or
  **delete** it from the viewer's menu.
