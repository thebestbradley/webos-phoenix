On a phone, the black strip below the screen is the **gesture area**. It
knows four moves:

- **Swipe up** from an app: the app shrinks into a card and you see all
  your cards. Swipe up again for the launcher.
- **Swipe left** (right to left): **back**. It goes back one step in the
  app, like a back button.
- **Swipe down** in card view: the card in the middle opens full screen.
- **Tap** it: switch between the app and card view.

The light bar in the gesture area glows when it sees a gesture.

## On a tablet

Tablets have no gesture area. **Flick up from the bottom edge** of the
screen for card view and the launcher. Apps have their own back buttons.

> **Tip:** in the simulator, Esc is back and Home (or F1) is a swipe up.
