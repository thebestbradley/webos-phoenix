- **Conversations** are listed newest first; the number shows how many
  are unread.
- **New message:** tap the compose button, type a name or number in
  **To:** and pick the person.
- **Reply:** type at the bottom of the conversation and tap send.

A new text arrives as a banner and in the notification area. Tap it to
open the conversation.
