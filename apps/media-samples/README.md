# Demo media

What Photos and Music find on a fresh device: seven photos, six short songs
on three albums, and the albums' covers. `media/` is mounted at
`/media/internal/samples` (`runtime/rootfs.json`), so the simulator and
`tools/install-rootfs.py` put it there.

Everything is generated by `tools/make-samples.cjs`, deterministically: the
pictures are drawn with canvas (landscapes; no people, no real photographs),
the songs are chiptunes synthesized by the script and encoded as Ogg Opus
(32 kbit/s mono). `media/index.json` lists what a media indexer would extract
from them; the simulated `com.webos.service.mediaindexer` reads it.

made up.
