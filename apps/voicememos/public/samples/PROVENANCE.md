# Voice Memos' demo memos

`memo-*.wav` and `samples.json` are made by `apps/voicememos/tools/make-samples.cjs`:
our own scripts, spoken by eSpeak NG (synthetic speech, no one's voice; eSpeak NG
runs as a program, and its GPL-3.0 does not cover the speech it outputs:
docs/LEGAL.md) and joined by ffmpeg. Dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0),
as the script's header says. Rerun the script after changing a script.
