CardDAV & CalDAV

Sync contacts and calendars with a standard server. Add the account in Accounts: tap Add an Account, then CardDAV & CalDAV.

Servers that work this way:

  • iCloud, Fastmail and other providers (with an app password)
  • Nextcloud and ownCloud
  • Radicale, Baïkal, SOGo and other self-hosted servers

Contacts show in Contacts, events in Calendar. Changes go both ways.
